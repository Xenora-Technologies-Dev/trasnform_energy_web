The Agency’s demand story is structural: industry, appliances, cooling, electric vehicles, heat pumps and data centres. It also records a fuel-price shock that raised LNG and wholesale electricity costs in several regions while leaving India’s prices comparatively less exposed, because LNG is a smaller part of the Indian generation mix.

On the supply side, solar continues to do most of the heavy lifting in annual growth. Gas-fired generation is expected to be broadly flat in 2026; coal-fired output rises in some systems as a short-term response to gas prices. That is a reminder that transition pathways are not linear year to year.

The operating implication is flexibility. The report notes persistent intraday spreads and, in some markets, a high share of negative-price hours — conditions that reward storage and demand response if market rules and interconnection allow them to operate. Grid modernisation and scheduling practice sit in that same gap.
