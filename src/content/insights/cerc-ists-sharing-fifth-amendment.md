The Commission’s notice (No. L-1/250/2019/CERC) invites comments, suggestions and objections on the draft by 31 August 2026, including through the SAUDAMINI e-regulation process.

The storage provisions are the most consequential change for hybrid and firm-and-dispatchable renewable designs. Waiver treatment would follow the generating station, provided the battery was included in connectivity. Charging from co-located renewable plant is treated differently from charging from other sources. Green Day Ahead Market energy could count toward the 51 percent renewable-charging requirement if it is certified and accounted as the draft requires.

Delay relief is narrower than a general extension. It is aimed at wind, solar and hybrid projects that had firm connectivity and then waited on the transmission system. The implementing agency would have 60 days after the amendment to publish revised detailed procedures — another reason the draft should be read as a direction of travel, not as an operating rule.
