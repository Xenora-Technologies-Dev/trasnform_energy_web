summary: "On 17 April 2026 the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission published the draft Power Market (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026. The draft would designate Grid Controller of India Limited as the sole Market Coupling Operator, with a separate cell to aggregate validated buy and sell bids from power exchanges and discover a uniform price for coupled segments. CERC had already decided, in 2025, to move toward market coupling in phases. The 2026 draft is the market-rule text that would put a single operator in the middle of that design. Grid-India would collect bids that exchanges have already received in a uniform format, validate, and pass across a secured channel according to the coupling procedure.

The governance overlay is easy to miss. Obligations that today sit on power exchanges — compliance, reporting, and related duties — would extend to the coupling operator. That is consistent with treating price discovery as a system function rather than a platform feature.

For scheduling and dispatch advisory, the practical questions are operational: when DAM and RTM actually couple, how bid submission windows change, how congestion splitting is published, and how state utilities and open-access consumers reconcile coupled prices with their existing scheduling tools. Those details will live in the procedure, not in the headline that Grid-India has been named.
